What a 12-Day Japan Trip Really Costs for 4 Friends (2026, Modeled Line by Line)

By Takumi, in Tokyo · Updated August 2026 · This is a transparent model at 2026 price levels, not one specific trip's receipts — every assumption is stated so you can bend it to your group. Flights excluded (they vary too much by origin to be honest about).

Budget threads are the most useful thing on Japan travel forums and the most frustrating: someone says "$1,500 was plenty," someone else says "$4,000 minimum," and both are describing their own trip, not yours. So instead of a number, here's the machine that produces the number — a mid-range 12-day Tokyo → Kyoto → Osaka trip for four friends sharing rooms, eating well but not extravagantly.

The assumptions (change these, and the total changes honestly)

  • 4 people, 2 twin rooms, mid-range hotels/business hotels (not hostels, not luxury)
  • 11 nights: 5 Tokyo, 3 Kyoto, 3 Osaka
  • One izakaya-style dinner most nights, casual lunches, konbini breakfasts
  • Shinkansen Tokyo→Kyoto, local trains Kyoto→Osaka, airport transfers
  • A few paid attractions and one splurge activity each; normal souvenir shopping

Three levers if the total scares you

  1. Nights out are the biggest swing. Two konbini-dinner nights a week saves ≈¥8,000/person over the trip without hurting the experience — save the izakaya budget for the nights that matter.
  2. Hotel class beats hotel city. Dropping from mid-range to business-hotel class saves more than switching neighborhoods; Japanese business hotels are clean, safe and everywhere.
  3. Skip the rail pass reflex. For this exact itinerary, point-to-point tickets beat the national pass at 2026 prices. Do the math for your route before buying any pass.
